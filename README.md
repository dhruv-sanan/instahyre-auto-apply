# Instahyre Auto-Apply

Browser console script that auto-applies to jobs on Instahyre.

## Usage

1. Open [Instahyre](https://www.instahyre.com) and go to your jobs/matches feed.
2. Open the browser DevTools console (F12 or Cmd+Option+J).
3. Paste the contents of `auto-apply.js` and press Enter.
4. It will click "Apply"/"Bulk Apply" buttons and open the next job card every ~3 seconds.
5. Run `stopAutoApply()` in the console to stop early.

Stops automatically after 100 applications or when no more jobs are found.

## Disclaimer

For personal use on your own account. Automating interactions with a site may violate its terms of service — use at your own risk.
